Office with refined characters | This round only replaces the boss and the assistant

1. Which file to open
In Blender choose File → Open and open office-character-detail.blend.
No script, add-ons or texture downloads are needed.
Made and verified with Blender 5.4.0 Alpha. Other versions have not been tested.

2. Viewing and rendering
1. Numpad 0: switch to the original main office camera; without a numpad use View → Cameras → Active Camera.
2. F12: re-render the 1600×1200 full view with the original camera and lights.
3. Middle-drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, Shift + middle-drag to pan.
4. After editing, use File → Save As so you don't overwrite the approved layout.

3. What changed this round
Male boss, based on IMG_6500.JPG: large round head, near-black short hair with a side-swept fringe, thick black brows, black dot eyes, peach skin with soft blush, small rose triangular nose, thin red mouth, dark slate-blue suit, white shirt and blue tie.
Female assistant, based on IMG_6499.JPG: dark brown-black shoulder-length side-parted hair, brown brows, black eyes with small lashes, peach skin with soft blush, small nose and thin red mouth, dark slate-blue suit and white shirt, no tie.
Both have ears, suit lapels and pocket edges, cuffs, buttons, fine fabric texture, bent seated trouser legs, shoe soles and stitching, separate fingers, thumbs and small nails; one hand holds a grey pen.
Original seats and workstation parenting are kept. These are static editable characters: no rig, animation or automatic expressions.
The 2D references only show the front; side, back and 3D proportions are designed from them and are not a strict 1:1 reconstruction.
To keep the original seated pose, the pen hand was not moved to the paper on the desk; the papers, keyboard and original pen are untouched.

4. Confirming nothing else changed
The original office-reference-layout.blend is kept, not overwritten, and the room script was not re-run.
645 non-character objects, 31 original materials, 10 collections, cameras, lights, world, floor, star garland, desks and chairs, computers, bookcase, window and desk items were compared before and after saving and after reopening.
Results are in character-refinement-scene-consistency-check.json; protected_changes should be an empty array.
The render output path points to the new preview file; resolution, samples, colour management, main camera and lighting are unchanged.
New shadows, occlusion and reflections from the characters' new shapes and materials are normal render changes, not furniture or lighting edits.

5. Finding and editing the characters
In the Outliner, expand 07_Pose_Placeholders (old collection name kept; it now holds the refined characters).
Boss root: Boss_POSE_ROOT / seated blockout only.
Assistant root: Assistant_POSE_ROOT / seated blockout only.
The old names are kept so roots match the verification, but the figures are no longer faceless placeholders.
Child objects are prefixed Boss character / and Assistant character /.
round face with soft chin: face.
short swept side-part hair cap: boss's short hair.
shoulder length side-part bob: assistant's shoulder-length hair.
tailored jacket body: suit body.
notched suit lapel: lapel.
blue tie knot / blue tie blade: boss's tie.
gray pen held in writing hand: the pen in hand.
Select a part and press Numpad . (period) to focus the view on it.
Select a character root and press G to move the whole character; select Boss_ROOT or Assistant_ROOT to move the full workstation, desk, chair and character together.
Character materials are named Character / ...; editing them doesn't affect the room or furniture Preview / materials.

6. Files
office-character-detail.blend: new editable office.
office-character-detail-preview.png: full view, original camera and lights.
boss-character-closeup.png: boss close-up check.
assistant-character-closeup.png: assistant close-up check.

office-character-refinement.zip: full bundle with the files above and the approved layout baseline.
Close-ups use temporary cameras for preview only; they are not saved into the final .blend, which keeps the original office camera.

7. Run the script only to rebuild the characters
Normally just open the .blend. The script is optional.
Put refine_office_characters.py in the same folder as office-reference-layout.blend.
Terminal command (macOS):
"/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender" --background --python-exit-code 1 --python "/full/path/refine_office_characters.py" -- --refine
/full/path is a placeholder; replace it with the script's actual location.
This reads the layout baseline and regenerates office-character-detail.blend and the full view, overwriting those outputs; the baseline is never overwritten.
Don't run it in a Blender window with unsaved changes: it loads the baseline and replaces the current scene. Save your work first.
